ABOUT THE PROJECT

Stenography, made easier to organise

StenoTrack is a simple web-based system for organising stenography and transcription assignments.

What is stenography?

Stenography is a method of writing quickly using symbols, abbreviations or specialised keyboard techniques. It is used in court reporting, meetings, interviews and transcription work.

Why StenoTrack?

Stenographers may need to manage different clients, deadlines, priorities and assignments. StenoTrack provides one place to record these details and monitor progress.
